# Doyle Blueprint And Balance

Develop a website for "Doyle Money Tree Enterprises LLC," an umbrella entity for three smaller businesses.
The company offers three specialized services: first, architectural engineering; second, stock trading strategy consulting; and third, Books & Bible Studies.

A website for the stock trading strategy service already exists at the domain tmt-tc.com. The architectural engineering service, as well as the Books & Bible Studies, will be featured primarily on this website.


Professional Services
A division of Doyle Money Tree Enterprises, LLC

Based in Tampa, Florida, we connect clients with professional knowledge and technical support to help move their projects forward.

We begin by listening—understanding what you want to accomplish, the challenges involved, and the assistance you need. From there, we explore how our team and professional collaborators can support your goals, with the scope and terms agreed upon before work begins.

Whether you have a developed project or an idea that needs direction, we welcome the opportunity to discuss it with you.

About the architectural engineering part:
1. My Vision for the Website
I would like to develop the Professional Services section as a dedicated division of the company that connects clients with architectural design, civil engineering, structural engineering, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, and plumbing) services.
The goal is to provide clients with a clear, professional point of contact where they can discuss their projects, explore available services, and receive personalized proposals based on their specific needs.
Rather than presenting the division as simply a design service, I would position it as a coordinated professional services team capable of supporting projects from the initial concept through design development and permit documentation.
2. Recommended Website Layout
A. Main Landing Page
The landing page should immediately explain what we offer and how we help clients.
Suggested headline:
Architectural Design & Engineering Solutions for Your Next Project
Supporting text:
From new construction to renovations, additions, and commercial projects, our team provides coordinated architectural design and civil, structural, and MEP engineering services tailored to each project's needs.
Whether you're starting with an idea or already have plans that need further development, we can help you identify the next steps and connect you with the appropriate professional expertise.
Primary buttons:
Discuss Your Project
Request a Quote
Explore Our Services
B. Our Services
I recommend dividing our services into four primary categories:
Architectural Design
Residential and commercial design
New construction
Home additions and renovations
Floor plans, elevations, and design development
3D visualization and conceptual design
Structural Engineering
Structural design and calculations
Framing and structural plans
Decks, additions, garages, and other structures
Structural assessments and design coordination
Civil Engineering
Site development coordination
Grading and drainage design
Site plans and related documentation
Coordination with other project professionals
MEP Engineering
Mechanical system design
Electrical plans
Plumbing design
Coordination of building systems with architectural and structural plans
The exact scope of each service would depend on the project, location, applicable requirements, and the qualifications of the professionals involved.
C. Meet the Professional Services Team
This section should introduce the people responsible for delivering the services and coordinating projects.
Each profile should include a professional photograph, title, short biography, areas of expertise, and relevant qualifications.
I suggest starting with two profiles:
President – Doyle Money Tree Enterprises, LLC
A brief introduction covering your leadership, company vision, business development, and commitment to connecting clients with professional services.
Vice President – Professional Services
My profile would focus on architectural design, civil and structural engineering coordination, MEP services, project development, and managing the technical process from the initial client discussion through the agreed deliverables.
As the division grows, we can add individual biographies for additional engineers, architects, designers, and other professional freelancers who join our network.
This would allow the website to grow naturally as we expand our capabilities.
D. Our Project Process
I recommend explaining our workflow in four straightforward steps:
Tell Us About Your Project
Clients describe their project, goals, location, and current stage of development.
Review and Define the Scope
We discuss the requirements, identify the services needed, and determine which professionals should be involved.
Receive a Project Proposal
The client receives a proposed scope of work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing for review and approval.
Move Forward with Professional Support
Once the terms are agreed upon, the assigned team begins the work and coordinates the agreed deliverables with the client.
This approach makes the process transparent and helps clients understand what to expect before committing to a project.
3. How the Website Should Work
I would make the website inquiry-driven rather than simply displaying a list of services.
The main goal should be to turn website visitors into qualified project inquiries.
The website should include:
Project Inquiry Form: Collect the client's name, contact information, project location, project type, and a short description of the work needed.
File Upload Option: Allow clients to provide existing plans, sketches, PDFs, photographs, or other relevant documents.
Service Selection: Let clients identify whether they need architectural, civil, structural, MEP, or combined services.
Consultation Request: Give clients an easy way to request an initial discussion.
Personalized Quotes: Review the project information and prepare a proposal tailored to its requirements.
Portfolio and Project Examples: Display selected work, sample deliverables, and relevant project experience as these materials become available.
I would also recommend making the website mobile-friendly and keeping the inquiry process simple, so potential clients can contact us without navigating through too many pages.
4. Future Expansion
As the division develops, we can expand the website to include:
Individual professional profiles and credentials
Additional architectural and engineering specialties
Residential and commercial project portfolios
Client testimonials and completed project examples
A frequently asked questions section
A project inquiry and lead-management system
The website should be structured so that adding new professionals and services does not require redesigning the entire platform.
5. My Recommendation
I would keep the Professional Services Division under the existing company brand while giving it a distinct identity, clear service categories, and a dedicated online presence.
The division should communicate three things to every potential client:
Professional expertise. Coordinated services. A clear path from concept to completion.
My role would be to help develop and manage the technical services side, coordinate the appropriate professionals, and ensure that clients understand the scope of services available to them.
Your role as President would focus on the overall company vision, leadership, and business development, while the Professional Services Division would provide a structured way to attract, assess, and manage technical project opportunities.
This structure would give us a solid foundation to launch the division, establish credibility, and expand the professional network over time.

Area	What visitors would see
Top banner	Company name and logo, with a simple menu: Home, Books & Bible Studies, Engineering Services, About Us, Contact
Welcome	A brief introduction explaining who you are and the work the company brings together

Visually, I picture a clean, readable design with generous space, clear headings, and a few purposeful images. Each section could have its own character while sharing the company’s logo and navigation.

The first page has one essential job: help visitors recognize where they belong and take their next step. The detail comes after they choose their entrance.
